## How do I use {{product}} on my phone today?
<!-- id: app-today-on-phone; page: /app; flags: app -->

Open your dashboard in your phone's browser. It is the same dashboard as on a computer, laid out for a small screen.

1. Open your phone's browser and go to the page where you sign in on your computer.
2. Sign in with the same email and password.
3. Open the menu to reach "Home", "Quotes", "Services", "Quote link" and "Settings".

You can approve a quote from your phone this way: open it under "Quotes" and press "Approve & send". The "Review and approve" email also has an "Approve & send" button that works on a phone.

## What will the phone app do?
<!-- id: app-what-it-does; page: /app; flags: app -->

{{#soon app}}
The iPhone app is {{soon}}. No business has it yet. This is what it is built to do:

- Start a "New quote" on site: pick the service, take the photos, answer that service's questions, add notes and the customer's name, then press "Price this job". Starting a quote will be part of {{onsite.plans}}. A new business will have it while it still has free quotes left.
- Work out the price from your own price list, while you are still there.
- Let you check the price and press "Approve & send", then send the customer the link to their quote.
- List your quotes, with the ones that need you first.
- Keep working with no signal: the quote is saved on the phone and sends by itself when the signal is back.

Everything except starting a quote will be open to every business, on any plan or none. It will not edit a quote's lines or your prices. That stays in the dashboard.
{{/soon}}
{{#live app}}
It prices a job while you are standing in it. Starting a quote is part of {{onsite.plans}}, and a new business has it while it still has free quotes left. Everything else in the app is open to every business, on any plan or none.

1. Open the app and tap "New quote".
2. Pick the service, take the photos and answer that service's questions.
3. Add notes and the customer's name, then press "Price this job". The price comes from your own price list.
4. Check the price and press "Approve & send". Then send the customer the link to their quote.

The "Quotes" tab lists your quotes, with the ones that need you first. With no signal, the quote is saved on the phone and sends by itself when the signal is back. The app does not edit a quote's lines or your prices: "Edit in the dashboard" opens the quote on the web.
{{/live}}

## A request could not be priced. Can I try again in the phone app?
<!-- id: app-try-again; page: /app/quotes/[quoteId]; flags: app -->

{{#soon app}}The phone app is {{soon}}. It will have the same "Try again" button as the quote's page on the website, which you can use today.{{/soon}}
{{#live app}}Yes. A request that says "Could not be priced" has a "Try again" button at the bottom of its screen in the app. It does the same as "Try again" on the website.

1. Open the "Quotes" tab and open the request.
2. Press "Try again". The screen shows the price being worked out again, from the same answers and photos, and then the price.
3. If it still cannot be priced, the screen says so again and you can press "Try again" once more.

Trying again does not use another quote from your plan. You can try one request up to five times, counting the tries made on the website. After that the button is gone and the screen says "This request has been tried 5 times and still has no price. Contact the customer and quote it yourself, or tell us with Help." The customer's email and phone number stay on the screen so you can reach them.{{/live}}

## What is the room scan?
<!-- id: app-room-scan; page: /app; flags: app, roomScan -->

{{#soon roomScan}}
The room scan is {{soon}}. It is part of the iPhone app, which is also {{soon}}, so no business can scan a room today. Today you type a room's sizes into the quote's questions yourself.

When it is live, you will walk the room with your phone's camera, in the app, and the room's sizes will go into the quote for you. It needs an iPhone that has a LiDAR scanner. It is included in {{roomScan.plans}}, and a new business will have it while it still has free quotes left.
{{/soon}}
{{#live roomScan}}
You walk the room with your phone's camera, in the iPhone app, and the room's sizes go into the quote for you. It needs an iPhone that has a LiDAR scanner. It is included in {{roomScan.plans}}, and a new business has it while it still has free quotes left. Without it, a scan is refused with the line "Room scan is part of Pro." Scans you already made stay on their quotes.

1. In the app, start a "New quote" and pick the service.
2. On the questions step, press "Scan an area" and walk the space with the camera.
3. Give the area a name and say how many rooms it covers. Press "Scan another area" for the next one.

Scanning one room at a time is the most accurate. A whole floor works too, but is less precise. The scan card shows only for a service whose price depends on a room's sizes.
{{/live}}

## What does a room scan fill in?
<!-- id: app-room-scan-fills; page: /app/quotes/[quoteId]; flags: app, roomScan -->

{{#soon roomScan}}
The room scan is {{soon}}, so nothing is filled in by a scan today.

When it is live, a scan will measure the floor area, the wall area with and without doors and windows, the ceiling, the length of the walls, and how many doors and windows there are. It will fill in the size questions of the service you are pricing. A number you typed yourself will be kept: the scan only fills what you left blank.
{{/soon}}
{{#live roomScan}}
A scan measures the floor area, the wall area with and without doors and windows, the ceiling, the length of the walls, and how many doors and windows there are. It fills in the size questions of the service you are pricing. A number you typed yourself is kept: the scan only fills what you left blank.

The quote's page in your dashboard shows a "Room scan" card with the sizes, a floor plan and a 3D model of the room. The card says which sizes priced the quote. While you are on your free quotes, a small "{{freeStart.plan}}" tag sits beside the card's title.
{{/live}}
