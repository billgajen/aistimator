# Your quote link, website form and QR code

## What is my quote link?
<!-- id: link-what-is; page: /app/quote-link -->

Your quote link is the web address of your own request page. A customer opens it, tells you about the job, adds photos and gets an estimate from your price list. You do not need a website to use it.

1. Open "Quote link" in the menu.
2. Your link is under "Your quote link".
3. Press "Open" to see the page your customers land on. It shows your business name at the top, with the words "Instant estimate".

Your link is also on Home, under "Your quote link", with the same "Copy" and "Open" buttons.

## How do I send my quote link to a customer?
<!-- id: link-copy-share; page: /app/quote-link -->

Copy the link and paste it anywhere you can type: a text message, an email, a chat or a social post. It is an ordinary link, so this works on every plan.

1. Open "Quote link".
2. Under "Your quote link", press "Copy". The button says "Copied".
3. Paste it where your customer will see it.

The customer taps the link and lands on your request page.

## Where should I put my quote link?
<!-- id: link-where-to-put; page: /app/quote-link -->

The "Where to put it" section has ready-made lines with your business name and your link already in them. Each has its own "Copy" button and a note saying where to paste it.

1. Open "Quote link" and scroll to "Where to put it".
2. Pick one: "Google Business Profile", "Facebook or Instagram bio", "Email signature" or "Text message reply".
3. Press "Copy" beside it.
4. Paste it in the place the note describes.

<!-- id: link-whatsapp; page: /app/quote-link; flags: whatsapp -->

## Can I change my quote link?
<!-- id: link-change-name; page: /app/quote-link -->

You can change the end of the link once. Your old link keeps working and sends people to the new one, so anything you already shared or printed is still good.

1. Open "Quote link".
2. Under your link, press "Change the end of the link".
3. Type the new ending under "New ending". It can use lowercase letters, numbers and single dashes.
4. Wait for "Available" to show. If the ending is taken or not allowed, a line in red says why.
5. Press "Save new link".

After that the page says "You've already changed this link once. Your old link still works." and the change is not offered again.

If your link still ends in random characters, you see "Get a cleaner link" in place of "Change the end of the link". Use that first: it is free, and your one change stays for later.

## My link ends in random letters. Can I get a cleaner one?
<!-- id: link-cleaner; page: /app/quote-link -->

Yes. If your link still ends in random characters, the page shows "Get a cleaner link". It is free and does not use up your one change.

1. Open "Quote link". The box "Get a cleaner link" is under your link.
2. Pick one of the endings offered, or choose "Type my own" and type one under "New ending".
3. Press "Use this link".

Your old link keeps working and sends people to the new one. If you do not see the box, your link is not a random one.

## How do I put the form on my website?
<!-- id: link-website-form; page: /app/quote-link -->

The same form can sit on your own website. You copy a short piece of code from the "Add it to your website" section and paste it into your site.

1. Open "Quote link" and scroll to "Add it to your website".
2. Choose a "Placement" and an "Experience" (the next entry explains them). The code in the dark box changes to match.
3. Press "Copy code".
4. Paste it into your website where it lets you add HTML or custom code. For "Inline (on your page)", paste it at the spot where the form should appear, for example on your contact page. For "Floating button", it can go anywhere on the page.
5. Save and publish your site, then open the page to check the form is there.

The code already has your business in it. Paste it exactly as copied.

If your site does not let you add code, add a button or a menu link that points to your quote link instead.

## What do "Placement" and "Experience" mean for the website form?
<!-- id: link-website-modes; page: /app/quote-link -->

They are the two choices above the code. Each has two options.

"Placement" is where the form shows:

- "Inline (on your page)": the form sits inside your page, at the spot where you paste the code.
- "Floating button": a button that says "Get an instant quote" stays in the bottom corner of your site. The form opens in a panel when a visitor presses it.

"Experience" is how the customer answers:

- "Step-by-step form": one short screen at a time.
- "AI chat (conversational)": the customer types messages in a chat, and it gathers the same details.

Pick one of each, then press "Copy code". If you change your choice later, copy the new code and replace the old code on your site.

## Someone else looks after my website. How do I get them the code?
<!-- id: link-web-person; page: /app/quote-link -->

Use "Send to my web person". It opens a new email in your own email program, with the code and plain instructions already written.

1. Open "Quote link" and scroll to "Add it to your website".
2. Choose the "Placement" and "Experience" you want.
3. Press "Send to my web person".
4. Add their email address and send it.

The email also gives them your quote link, in case adding code is not possible on your site.

## How do I check what the form looks like?
<!-- id: link-preview; page: /app/quote-link -->

There are two ways.

- Press "Open" beside your quote link. This is your real request page, as your customers see it.
- Under "Add it to your website", press "Preview on a demo page". A page called "Widget Demo" opens in a new tab. Choose a "Placement" and an "Experience" there and press "Load Widget" to see your form as it would sit on a website.

Both show your real form. A request you send from either one is a real request, and it shows in Quotes like any other.

## How do I get a QR code for my quote link?
<!-- id: link-qr-code; page: /app/quote-link -->

The "QR code" section shows a QR code for your quote link. Customers point their phone camera at it and land on your request page.

1. Open "Quote link" and find "QR code".
2. Press "Download PNG". A picture file is saved to your device.
3. Print it, or send the file to your printer or sign maker. It works on a van, flyers, business cards or invoices.

There is no print button on the page. You print the downloaded picture yourself.

If you change your link, the page shows a code for the new link. A code you already printed still works, because the old link sends people to the new one.

## What does my customer do on the form?
<!-- id: link-customer-steps; page: /app/quote-link -->

They go through a few short steps. The form leaves out a step your setup does not need.

1. "What do you need?": they pick one of your services. This step is skipped if you have one service.
2. "Where should we send your estimate?": they give "Your name", "Email" and "Phone". Which of email and phone they must give is set in Settings, under "Customer contact details".
3. "About the job": they answer the questions you set for that service, and give a quantity if the service asks for one. This step is skipped if the service asks for neither.
4. "Photos & Documents": they add photos of the job. Whether photos are required depends on the service.
5. "Last step": they give their postcode or ZIP code and can write more under "Anything else about the job?". Then they press "Get my estimate".

If the service asks no questions, the customer must describe the job in the last step.

With "AI chat (conversational)" the customer gives the same details in a chat instead of steps.

Once a customer has finished the contact step, their details are saved. If they stop there, you can see them in Quotes under "Unfinished".

## What does my customer see after they send the request?
<!-- id: link-after-submit; page: /app/quote-link -->

They see "Preparing your estimate". It usually takes a minute or two. What comes next depends on the service's setting for when estimates are sent.

- If the estimate can be shown, a customer who used your quote link is taken straight to their estimate page. On your website form, they see the total and its lines, with a "View your estimate" button.
- If a detail is needed to price the job, they see "One quick check" and are asked to confirm it.
- If the estimate waits for you, they see "Your estimate is being prepared" and that you will review and send it shortly.

A customer who gave an email address is also emailed a link to their estimate when it is ready for them.

## Can I add my own questions to the form?
<!-- id: link-extra-questions; page: /app/quote-link -->

Yes. The "Advanced" section at the bottom of the Quote link page holds extra questions for the form.

1. Open "Quote link" and open "Advanced".
2. Press "Add Field".
3. Type the question under "Label" and choose a "Field Type", for example "Dropdown" or "Yes/No Toggle".
4. For a question with choices, fill in "Options". Each choice needs a short value and the words the customer sees.
5. Under "Show for Service", leave "All Services (Global)" to ask it for every service, or pick one service.
6. Tick "Required field" if the customer must answer.
7. Press "Add Field", then "Save Changes".

The question then shows on the form's "About the job" step.

## What counts as "Share your quote link" on Home?
<!-- id: link-shared-step; page: /app -->

The setup step "Share your quote link" on Home is ticked the first time either of these happens:

- You copy your link. That means "Copy" beside your quote link (on Home or on the Quote link page), or "Copy" on any of the ready-made texts on the Quote link page.
- Your request page is opened for the first time. Pressing "Open" yourself counts.

Copying the website code with "Copy code" does not tick it. A test request you make during setup does not tick it either.
