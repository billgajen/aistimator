# Your account, branding and notifications

## How do I change my business name, region, currency or units?
<!-- id: account-business-info; page: /app/settings; section: Business Information -->

They are under "Business Information" on the "Business" tab of "Settings".

1. Open "Settings". The "Business" tab opens first.
2. Change "Business Name", "Region", "Currency" or "Units".
3. Press "Save Business Info".

- "Region" is "United States" or "United Kingdom". It sets the currency, the units, the usual name of the tax, and whether customers are asked for a ZIP code or a postcode. Choosing a region puts "Currency" and "Units" back to that region's own. You can change them again before you save.
- "Currency" takes any three-letter currency code. It changes the currency your prices are shown in. It does not convert the numbers in your price list.
- "Units" is "Imperial (feet, sq ft, gallons)", "Metric (metres, sqm, litres)" or the region's own.
- "Contact Email" is the email you sign in with. It cannot be changed here.

## How do I add tax to my quotes?
<!-- id: account-tax; page: /app/settings; section: Tax Settings -->

Switch it on under "Tax Settings" and give the rate. Tax is added on top: the prices in your price list are before tax.

1. Open "Settings". Under "Tax Settings", tick "Enable tax on quotes".
2. In "Tax Rate (%)", type the rate as a percentage.
3. In "Tax Label", type what the tax is called on your quotes. Leave it empty to use your region's usual name.
4. Press "Save Tax Settings".

The customer's estimate then shows "Subtotal", a line for the tax, and "Total". With the box unticked there is no tax line.

A new rate applies to estimates priced after you save. An estimate already made keeps its tax unless it is priced again.

## How do I limit where I take jobs?
<!-- id: account-service-area; page: /app/settings; section: Service Area -->

Under "Service Area" you can list the ZIP codes or postcodes you cover. A customer outside them cannot send a request.

1. Open "Settings". Under "Service Area", choose "Postcode/ZIP allowlist".
2. In "Allowed Postcodes/ZIP Codes", type your codes, separated by commas or one on each line.
3. Press "Save Service Area".

- A customer's code passes when it starts with one of yours. So the first part of a code covers every code that starts with it.
- A customer outside your list sees "Location is outside service area" when they send the form. Their request is not sent and no estimate is made.
- The check uses the code the customer types. A customer who leaves the code empty is not checked.
- "No restrictions" takes requests from anywhere.
- "County/State" saves your list, but today nothing is checked against it. Nobody is turned away. Use "Postcode/ZIP allowlist" to limit your area.

## What does the "Quote Template" section change?
<!-- id: account-quote-template; page: /app/settings; section: Quote Template -->

Today it changes the PDF of a quote, not the quote page your customer opens online.

- "Show line items", "Include assumptions" and "Include exclusions" decide whether those parts are printed on the PDF. The online quote page always shows the price lines, assumptions and exclusions a quote has.
- "Quote Validity (days)" sets the valid-until date on the PDF, counted from the day the quote was made. It takes a number from 1 to 365.
- "Default Terms & Conditions" is saved, but today it is not printed on the quote page or on the PDF. For a note at the bottom of every quote, use "Footer Notes" on the "Branding" tab.

1. Open "Settings". Under "Quote Template", tick the parts you want and set the days.
2. Press "Save Quote Template".

The same three tick boxes and the validity days are also on the "Branding" tab. Both places change the same settings.

If you have unticked "Allow customers to accept quotes directly" on the "Branding" tab, check it after you press "Save Quote Template". Saving here switches it back on.

## Which contact details must my customers give?
<!-- id: account-customer-contact; page: /app/settings; section: Customer contact details -->

You choose under "Customer contact details". A name is always asked for.

1. Open "Settings". Under "Customer contact details", choose one:
   - "Email required, phone optional"
   - "Phone required, email optional"
   - "Both email and phone required"
2. Press "Save contact settings".

Customers give these before the job's questions, so you can reach them even if they do not finish.

## What is the ID under "Account Information"?
<!-- id: account-info; page: /app/settings; section: Account Information -->

"Account Information" shows one line, "Tenant ID". It is the reference number of your business in {{product}}. It is there to read only. Nothing in this section can be changed.

## How do I delete my account?
<!-- id: account-delete; page: /app/settings; section: Danger Zone -->

You ask the team by email. The dashboard has no button that deletes an account.

1. Open "Settings" and go to "Danger Zone" at the bottom of the "Business" tab.
2. Press "Delete your account". It opens the "Delete your data" page, which sets out the steps. Nothing is deleted when you press it.
3. Email {{support.email}} from the email address of your account. Say that you want your whole account closed.

When an account is closed, your quote history stays available for export for 30 days. Then it is deleted: contact details, requests and their answers, photos and documents, and messages.

If you have a paid plan, cancel it first on the "Billing" tab, or ask for that in the same email.

## How do I change my email or my password?
<!-- id: account-email-password; page: /app/settings -->

You change your password from the sign-in page. The email you sign in with cannot be changed in the dashboard.

To change your password:

1. Press "Sign out" in the menu.
2. On the sign-in page, press "Forgot your password?".
3. Type your email and press "Send the link".
4. Open the link in the email. It works once, for one hour.
5. Type a new password of at least 8 characters and press "Save the new password".

To change the email you sign in with, email {{support.email}} from your current address.

If you only want notifications to go to another address, set it on the "Notifications" tab.

## Can I give someone else on my team a login?
<!-- id: account-team-logins; page: /app/settings -->

No. Today a business has one login. There is no way to invite a second person.

## How do I add my logo?
<!-- id: account-branding-logo; page: /app/settings/branding -->

Upload it on the "Branding" tab. It is saved as soon as it has uploaded.

1. Open "Settings", then the "Branding" tab.
2. Under "Logo", press "Click to upload your logo" and choose the file. It must be a PNG, JPG or WebP of 2MB or less.
3. Look at "Preview" on the right to see it on a quote.

Your logo shows on your request form, on the quote page and on the PDF, beside your business name. Emails to your customers carry your business name and your colour, not the logo.

To change it press "Replace". To take it off press "Remove".

## How do I change my colour and the details customers see on a quote?
<!-- id: account-branding-details; page: /app/settings/branding -->

They are on the "Branding" tab. Press "Save Changes" at the bottom when you are done.

1. Open "Settings", then the "Branding" tab.
2. Under "Brand Colors", pick a colour for "Primary Color", or type its code (a # and six characters). It is used for buttons, links and accents on your request form and your quotes. The text on top of it is chosen for you so it stays readable.
3. Under "Business Details", fill in "Tagline", "Phone", "Email" and "Website". They show under your name on the quote page and the PDF. The tagline also shows on your request form.
4. Under "Footer Notes", type any text for the bottom of your quotes.
5. Under "Quote Template", tick "Allow customers to accept quotes directly" to put an accept button on the quote page. Untick it to leave the button off.
6. Press "Save Changes".

"Preview" on the right shows a sample quote with your changes before you save.

## Can I remove "Prepared with {{product}}" from my quotes?
<!-- id: account-badge; page: /app/settings/branding -->

Yes, on {{badge.plans}}. It is removed for you. There is no switch.

- On those plans the quote page no longer says "Prepared with {{product}}", and the request form no longer says "Powered by {{product}}".
- Emails to your customers still say "Powered by {{product}}" at the bottom, on every plan.
- The PDF of a quote does not carry the line on any plan.
- The line comes back if your plan ends, and while a payment that failed is being tried again.

## Where do notifications go, and how do I change the address?
<!-- id: account-notifications-email; page: /app/settings/notifications -->

They go to one email address. Unless you set another, it is the email of your account.

1. Open "Settings", then the "Notifications" tab.
2. In "Send notifications to", type the address.
3. Press "Save Notifications".

Leave the field empty to go back to your account's email.

{{product}} emails that address when:

- a customer's request has been priced, or is waiting for you to approve it;
- a request was saved without a price because you had no quotes left;
- a request could not be priced;
- a customer accepts or declines a quote, asks about it, or changes their request;
- your free quotes are nearly used, your quotes have run out, or a payment failed.

The same address is where your customers' replies go when they answer an email about their quote.

There are no switches for single emails. You choose the address, not which emails are sent.
