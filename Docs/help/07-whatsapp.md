# WhatsApp

## Can my customers get a quote through WhatsApp today?
<!-- id: whatsapp-share-link-today; page: /app/quote-link; flags: whatsapp -->

Yes, by link, on every plan. Send your quote link in any WhatsApp chat. The customer taps it, fills in your request form and gets their estimate on its own page.

1. Open "Quote link" in the menu.
2. Find "Use it on WhatsApp" and press "Send on WhatsApp".
3. WhatsApp opens with a message that has your link in it. Pick the chat and send.

## How do I connect my WhatsApp number?
<!-- id: whatsapp-connect; page: /app/whatsapp; flags: whatsapp -->

{{#soon whatsapp}}
You cannot yet. Connecting a number is {{soon}}. Today, send your quote link in your chats instead: open "Quote link" and use "Use it on WhatsApp".
{{/soon}}
{{#live whatsapp}}
Connect it on the "WhatsApp" page. It takes a few minutes. Your plan has to include it ({{whatsapp.plans}}), or you are a new business that still has free quotes left.

1. Open "WhatsApp" in the menu.
2. Press "Connect WhatsApp". Meta, the company behind WhatsApp, opens in a small window.
3. In that window, sign in, choose your business and pick a number. You can use a new number, or the one you already use in the WhatsApp Business app, which keeps working there. Press Finish on the last screen.
4. Back on the page you see "Connected" and your number. If the page says "One step left" with "Finish registering the number", press "Register the number". Enter the number's two-step PIN if it has one, otherwise leave the box empty.
5. Under "Try it", first send "Hi" to your business number from your own phone. Then type your own mobile number, with country code, and press "Send a test message".

Use your business WhatsApp number. Do not connect a personal number: it can stop working in the normal WhatsApp app. You can close Meta's window at any point without changing anything.
{{/live}}
