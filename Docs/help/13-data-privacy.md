# Your data and privacy

## What does {{product}} keep about my business?
<!-- id: privacy-business-data; page: /app/settings -->

The Privacy Policy lists it:

- the email you sign in with;
- your business settings: your services, prices, branding and the other things you set up;
- your billing details. Payment cards are handled by Stripe and never reach {{product}}'s servers;
- how you found {{product}} when you signed up: the site you came from, any campaign tags on the link, and the first page you opened;
- basic technical logs (network address and times), for security and to limit abuse.

## What is kept about my customers?
<!-- id: privacy-customer-data; page: /app/settings -->

What they send when they ask you for a price:

- their name and contact details (email and phone);
- the address of the job;
- the photos and documents they upload;
- their answers to your questions, and any message they write.

This data belongs to you and your customers. {{product}} handles it only to provide the service to you, and does not sell it. You decide what is collected from your customers, and you are responsible for having a lawful reason to collect it.

## Where are my customers' photos stored, and who can see them?
<!-- id: privacy-photos; page: /app/settings -->

Photos and documents are stored privately. They can be reached only through a link that works for a limited time, or through the quote's own page.

- You see the photos of your own customers' requests.
- No other business can see your customers' details or photos.
- Photos, files and the full text of messages are not written to {{product}}'s logs.

## How is AI used, and does it set my prices?
<!-- id: privacy-ai; page: /app/settings -->

AI reads the photos and the words of a request to work out the job's details and measurements. It also drafts wording. The price is always worked out from your own price list.

- Photos are used only to prepare the estimate.
- The measurements taken from photos, never the photos, can be used to make your own later estimates more accurate.
- Your customers' photos are not used to train public AI models.

## Which other companies handle the data?
<!-- id: privacy-companies; page: /app/settings; flags: whatsapp -->

{{product}} uses these companies, each only for the job named. The Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service carry the same list.

- Supabase: the database and sign-in, held in the EU.
- Cloudflare: storing photos, documents and PDFs, working out each request, and the check that tells people from bots.
- Vercel: hosting the website, the dashboard and the quote pages.
- Google: the AI models that read a request's photos and words.
- Postmark: sending email.
- DocRaptor: making the PDF of a quote.
- Upstash: counting requests by network address for a few minutes, to stop abuse.
- Stripe: payment for your own subscription. Stripe never gets your customers' details.

## How long is the data kept?
<!-- id: privacy-how-long; page: /app/settings -->

For as long as your account is open, so that you and your customer can both come back to a quote.

When an account is closed, its quote history stays available for export for 30 days. Then it is deleted.

Messages you send to Help, and your questions to the assistant with its answers, are kept to answer you and to improve support. Questions to the assistant and their answers are deleted after {{ask.keepDays}} days.

## How do I delete one customer's data?
<!-- id: privacy-delete-customer; page: /app/settings -->

You ask the team. The dashboard has no button that deletes a customer's request.

1. Email {{support.email}} from the email address of your account.
2. Say which customer's request you want deleted.

A deletion covers the personal data held for that request: contact details, the request and its answers, photos and documents, and messages.

## How do I delete my whole account and its data?
<!-- id: privacy-delete-account; page: /app/settings -->

You ask the team by email. Nothing is deleted from the dashboard itself.

1. Open "Settings". At the bottom of the "Business" tab, under "Danger Zone", press "Delete your account". It opens the "Delete your data" page with the steps.
2. Email {{support.email}} from the email address of your account. Say that you want your whole account closed.

Your quote history stays available for export for 30 days after the account is closed. Then it is deleted.

## A customer asked me to see, correct or delete their data. What do I do?
<!-- id: privacy-customer-request; page: /app/settings -->

You are the one responsible for your customers' data, so the request comes to you first.

- To see what a customer sent, open their request in "Quotes".
- To delete it, email {{support.email}} from your account's address and say which customer's request it is.
- A customer can also email {{support.email}} themselves, from the address they used for the request, and say which business it was. The team passes the request on to you, or acts on it where it can.

## Do my customers get tracking cookies from my quote pages?
<!-- id: privacy-cookies; page: /app/settings -->

No advertising tracking runs on your quote pages, your request form or the dashboard. The Privacy Policy says the advertising measurement is used only on {{product}}'s own public pages.

- The dashboard uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.
- When a customer sends a request, the check that tells people from bots may set one cookie from Cloudflare. It is used only for that.

## How is the data protected, and what happens if there is a breach?
<!-- id: privacy-security; page: /app/settings -->

The Terms of Service say:

- photos and documents are stored privately;
- each business can read only its own customers' data;
- data is encrypted on its way to and from {{product}};
- logs are kept to the minimum.

If {{product}} learns of a security breach that affects your customers' data, it will tell you without undue delay, by email to your account's address, with what is known.

## Who do I ask about privacy, or for a data-processing agreement?
<!-- id: privacy-contact; page: /app/settings -->

Email {{support.email}}.

- Ask there for anything about your data or your customers' data.
- If your business needs a written data-processing agreement, ask for one at the same address.
- The full wording is in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service, on {{product}}'s public site. This guide does not replace them and is not legal advice.
