# Getting help: asking a question, reporting a problem, suggesting a feature

## How do I ask a question about using {{product}}?
<!-- id: help-ask; page: /app -->

Use "Ask {{product}}", the first thing in the Help panel. It is an AI assistant that answers from this help guide. It can be wrong, so check anything important.

1. Press "Help" at the bottom of the menu, above "Sign out".
2. Type your question in the box under "Ask {{product}}" and press "Ask". You can also press one of the questions under "You could ask".
3. Read the answer. A button under it, such as "Open Services", opens the page the answer is about.
4. Under the answer, "Did this answer it?" takes "Yes" or "No". "No" offers "Send this to the {{product}} team".

If your question is one word, or could mean several things, the assistant does not guess. It asks "Do you mean one of these? If not, tell me a little more." and shows two or three questions. Press the one you mean, or type a fuller question.

Your conversation stays in the panel until you sign out. "Start again" clears it. You can ask {{ask.dailyQuestions}} questions a day. A question the assistant asks back about does not count.

## What can the assistant do, and what can it not do?
<!-- id: help-ask-limits; page: /app -->

It reads and answers. It does not change anything.

- It answers from this guide and from your own setup: your plan, how many quotes you have used, your services, and the page you are on.
- Asked from a quote's page, it can explain how that quote's price was worked out, line by line.
- It never changes a price, a setting, a service or a quote, and it never sends anything to your customers. Its buttons only open a page or a form.
- When it does not know, it says "I’m not sure about that." and offers "Send this to the {{product}} team".
- When a question is one word or unclear, it asks which of two or three things you mean before it answers.
- It does not advise on refunds, payment disputes, or legal and tax questions. It passes those to the team.
- It answers in English.

Your questions and its answers are kept for {{ask.keepDays}} days, to answer you and to improve support. Then they are deleted.

## How do I send a question to the team when the assistant cannot answer?
<!-- id: help-send-to-team; page: /app -->

Press "Send this to the {{product}} team" under the answer. Your conversation is sent with it, so you do not have to type it again.

1. In the Help panel, under the answer, press "Send this to the {{product}} team".
2. The panel says "Thanks, we’ve got it. You’ll see our reply here and by email."
3. Our reply shows under "Your reports and requests", and is emailed to you.

## How do I report a problem?
<!-- id: help-report-problem; page: /app -->

Use "Report a problem" in the Help panel. It is for something that broke or looks wrong.

1. Press "Help" at the bottom of the menu.
2. Press "Report a problem".
3. Under "What went wrong?", say what happened.
4. To add a picture, use "Add a screenshot (optional)". It must be an image of up to 10 MB.
5. Read the list under "We’ll include". It shows what is sent with your report.
6. Press "Send report".

The panel says "Thanks, we’ve got it. You’ll see our reply here and by email." Your report is saved even if the screenshot cannot be added.

## What is sent with a problem report?
<!-- id: help-what-is-sent; page: /app -->

Only what the form lists under "We’ll include", and you see the list before you send:

- the page you were on;
- the quote, when the page is a quote's own page;
- your plan;
- your browser and the size of your screen;
- the last error the page showed, if there was one.

Nothing else from the page is sent.

## How do I report an estimate that looks wrong?
<!-- id: help-report-estimate; page: /app/quotes/[quoteId] -->

Use "Report a problem with this estimate", at the bottom of that quote's own page. It tells the team about that one estimate. It changes nothing on the estimate and nothing in your prices.

1. Open "Quotes" and open the quote.
2. Scroll to the bottom and press "Report a problem with this estimate".
3. Under "What is wrong? Choose one or more.", tick what fits: "Price too high", "Price too low", "Wrong measurement or reading", "Missed something the customer asked for", "Included something it shouldn’t", "Wording" or "Something else".
4. Add detail under "Tell us more". It is required for "Something else".
5. "What should the total have been?" is optional. It is for our records only. It does not change this or any other price.
6. Press "Send report".

To correct the estimate itself for your customer, press "Edit" on the quote's page, change it and press "Save & Send".

## How do I know an estimate has been reported, and what happened to my report?
<!-- id: help-estimate-reported; page: /app/quotes/[quoteId] -->

The quote's page shows one line at the bottom, such as "Reported on" with the date, and the report's status beside it. If the quote was reported more than once, the line says how many times and gives the date and status of the latest report.

Sometimes a note shows under it that starts "This may be down to your prices:". It names one of your own price lines and what it is set to. It is a possibility for you to check on "Services", not a finding, and nothing has been changed.

The full report, with our replies, is in the Help panel under "Your reports and requests".

## How do I suggest a feature?
<!-- id: help-suggest-feature; page: /app -->

Use "Suggest a feature" in the Help panel.

1. Press "Help" at the bottom of the menu.
2. Press "Suggest a feature".
3. Under the question that asks what you would like {{product}} to do, say what you want, and press "Send".
4. If others have asked for something similar, you are shown up to three requests. Press "Yes, add my vote" on the one you mean, or "No, mine is different" to send yours as a new request.

Each business has one vote on a request. You are not shown who else asked, or how many. The team reads every request; a request is not a promise that it will be built.

## Where do I see my reports and requests, and the replies?
<!-- id: help-your-reports; page: /app -->

In the Help panel, under "Your reports and requests". Everyone who signs in to your business sees the same list.

1. Press "Help" at the bottom of the menu.
2. Press "Your reports and requests".
3. Press a report to open it. Our replies are there, with a box to answer.

A report's status is "Sent", "Looking into it", "Fixed", "Answered" or "Not a fault". A feature request's status is "Under review", "Planned", "Shipped" or "Not planned".

When we reply, a "New" mark shows beside "Help" in the menu until someone at your business opens the report. We also email the person who sent it.

## Is there a limit on reports and requests?
<!-- id: help-limits; page: /app -->

Yes: {{support.dailyReports}} reports and {{support.dailyReports}} feature requests a day for each business, and {{ask.dailyQuestions}} questions a day to the assistant. If you reach a limit, try again the next day or email {{support.email}}.

## Can I email instead?
<!-- id: help-email; page: /app -->

Yes. Email {{support.email}}. The address is also at the bottom of the Help panel.

For anything about your account, your plan or your data, write from the email address you sign in with.

## Is there a support page I can open without signing in?
<!-- id: help-support-page; page: /app; flags: app -->

Yes. On the {{product}} website, press "Support" at the bottom of any page. It needs no sign-in.

It says how to reach us ({{support.email}}, and "Ask {{product}}" in this Help panel), and it links to the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service and the page for deleting your account.
