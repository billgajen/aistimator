# Templates

## What is a template?
<!-- id: templates-what; page: /app/services -->

A template is a ready-made set of services for one trade. Each service comes with what the job includes, the questions to ask the customer, the photos to ask for, and typical prices.

A template saves you building a service from nothing. Its prices are a starting point, not your prices: you check them and change them to yours.

You meet templates in two places: the first screen of setup ("What kind of work do you do?"), and "Add from a template" on the "Services" page.

## Which templates are there?
<!-- id: templates-list; page: /app/services -->

There are ten today. You only see the ones for the country your business is set to.

For a business in the United States:

- "Water heaters (US)": Water Heater Replacement.
- "HVAC (US)": Central AC Replacement, Heat Pump Replacement, Gas Furnace Replacement, Ductless Mini-Split Installation.
- "Electrical (US)": Electrical Panel Upgrade, EV Charger Installation.
- "Plumbing fixtures (US)": Fixture Replacement.
- "Garage doors (US)": Garage Door Replacement, Garage Door Spring Replacement.
- "Painting (US)": Interior Painting, Exterior Painting, Wallpaper Installation.
- "Fence installation (US)": Fence Installation.
- "Junk removal (US)": Junk Removal.
- "Pressure washing (US)": Pressure Washing.

For a business in the United Kingdom:

- "Painting & Decorating (UK)": Interior Painting, Exterior Painting, Wallpapering.

## How do I start from a template when I first set up?
<!-- id: templates-setup; page: /app/onboarding -->

Pick your trade on the first screen of setup. Its services are filled in for you, and you check the prices before anything is created.

1. On "What kind of work do you do?", check "Where is your business?", pick your trade and press "Continue".
2. "Here's what we found" lists the template's services, all ticked. Untick any you do not offer, then press "Looks right — continue".
3. On "Check your prices", a note says "These are typical starting prices. Change them to yours." Change any number, then press "These are my prices".
4. Finish "Last step — the basics". Your services are created.

Setup takes one trade. "Start over" on the services screen takes you back to choose again. To add services from another trade, use "Add from a template" on "Services" afterwards.

## How do I add a service from a template later?
<!-- id: templates-add; page: /app/services -->

Use "Add from a template" on the "Services" page. You add one service at a time.

1. Open "Services" and press "Add from a template". If you have no services yet, the link reads "Or add one from a template".
2. Find the service under its trade. Each one shows a starting price and a short description.
3. Press "Add" beside it.
4. The service is added to your list, switched off, and opens at "Pricing Setup" with a note that these are the template's example prices. Check every price there and change them to yours.
5. Press "Next" to reach "Test & Publish", then press "Save Changes".
6. Back on "Services", press "Enable" beside the service.

A service added from a template starts "Inactive". Your website form and quote link do not offer it until you press "Enable", so no customer is quoted the template's example prices. A note at the top of "Services" reminds you until you switch it on.

## Are a template's prices my prices?
<!-- id: templates-prices; page: /app/services -->

No. They are typical starting prices, and you must check them. Your estimates charge whatever numbers are in the service, so change them to what you charge.

- In setup, "Check your prices" shows every number with the note "These are typical starting prices. Change them to yours." Setup does not go on until you press "These are my prices".
- In "Add from a template", the screen says "A service you add starts switched off." The service opens for you to check its prices, and you press "Enable" on "Services" when they are yours. The price shown beside each service in the list is the template's starting price.

Pressing "These are my prices" and finishing setup, or saving a service on "Services", ticks "Check your prices" on "Home". Adding a template's service does not tick it until you save the service.

## Can I add a second template?
<!-- id: templates-second; page: /app/services -->

Yes. Setup takes one trade, but "Add from a template" on "Services" lists every template for your country, and you can add services from any of them.

1. Open "Services" and press "Add from a template".
2. Scroll to the other trade and press "Add" beside each service you want, one at a time.
3. Check the prices of each one as it opens, save it, then press "Enable" beside it on "Services".

For example, a heating and cooling business that takes on water heaters can add "Water Heater Replacement" this way. The services you already have are not changed.

## What does "You have this" mean on a template?
<!-- id: templates-have-this; page: /app/services -->

It means you already have a service with that name, so there is nothing to add. The "Add" button is replaced by "You have this".

The check is by the service's name only, ignoring capital letters and spacing. If you renamed your own service, the template's service shows "Add" again, and adding it gives you a second, separate service.

## Can I change a service after adding it from a template?
<!-- id: templates-change; page: /app/services -->

Yes, everything. Once it is added, a service from a template is an ordinary service of yours.

1. Open "Services" and press "Edit" beside the service.
2. Go through the steps with "Next": "Basic Info", "Scope & Photos", "Customer Questions", "Pricing Setup" and "Test & Publish". Change the name, what is included, the questions, the photos asked for and the prices.
3. On "Test & Publish", press "Save Changes".

To stop offering a service without losing it, press "Disable". "Enable" switches it back on. "Delete" removes it.

## What if there is no template for my trade?
<!-- id: templates-none; page: /app/services -->

You can still set up your own services. A template is a shortcut, not a requirement.

- In setup, pick "Something else". {{product}} reads your website or your own description and suggests your services.
- After setup, press "Add Service" on "Services" and build the service yourself.

If "Add from a template" says "There are no templates for your region yet.", none has been written for your country so far.
